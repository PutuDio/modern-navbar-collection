About Frequency Studio

Frequency Studio is an experimental audio-visual research collective exploring the intersection of spatial audio design, browser-native synthesis, and interaction design. Our work lives entirely in the browser — no plugins, no DAW, just the Web Audio API and a vision for what navigation could sound like.
